Research question

How is population growth associated with rental pressure across NSW LGAs?

Read the NSW-wide evidence first, then compare one selected LGA either against the NSW median or against another LGA.

Statewide evidence

The association varies materially by dwelling type

Houses, flats and units show a positive and statistically significant relationship. The townhouse relationship is much weaker and not statistically significant.

These NSW-wide results stay the same when you change the selected LGA below.

2021–2025 growth × 2025 rent

How to read this

The ranked bars show the associated rent increase per additional percentage point of population growth, expressed as a percentage of that dwelling type’s NSW median rent.

Why normalise

Normalisation lets dwelling types be compared without higher-priced categories appearing stronger only because their rents are measured in larger dollar amounts.

Answer

Population growth is positively associated with higher rents, but the strength is not uniform. The clearest evidence is for houses, flats and units; townhouses do not show a reliable statewide relationship in this dataset.
