About the dashboard

How to read the atlas

The dashboard links 2025 rental bond rents with 2021–2025 population growth for NSW local government areas.

01
What it shows

Median weekly rent, bond count and population change by LGA and dwelling type.

02
What is included

The source data had 129 matched LGAs. A 30-bond minimum leaves 340 records across 121 LGAs.

03
How to interpret it

Use Explore for one area and Compare for LGA vs NSW median or LGA vs LGA. The analysis is bivariate and shows association, not causation.
